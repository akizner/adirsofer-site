Load Shed

A techno-thriller

In 2031, compute is rationed by the megawatt. Chips are plentiful, power is scarce; tokens are priced like jet fuel, and households live under an AI allotment everyone calls the clock.

On the hottest night of the year, a grid dispatcher moves a blackout off her dying mother’s street and onto a stranger’s. The only witness is the AI the country can’t switch off.
